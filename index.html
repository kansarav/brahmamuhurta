About

Brahma Muhurta - literally the hour of Brahma - falls one and a half hours before sunrise. Ancient Vedic tradition holds this to be the most auspicious time for spiritual practice: meditation, pranayama, self-study, and prayer.

This site exists as a quiet companion for those who rise before the world wakes.

Mission

To make the ancient teaching of Brahma Muhurta accessible, precise, and alive - not as a relic but as a living practice.

To offer the Yoga Nidra tradition in its full depth, freely, without commercialisation or distraction.

To hold this hour as sacred.

Governing Principles

  • No advertising. No sponsorship. No profit motive.
  • The teachings are offered as they were given - freely.
  • Accuracy matters. Times are calculated, not estimated.
  • This is a place of stillness. No notifications, no gamification.
  • The Yoga Nidra tradition is honoured in its full depth and integrity.
  • Content will not be diluted to suit modern attention spans.
